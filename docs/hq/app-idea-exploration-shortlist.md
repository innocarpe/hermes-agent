# App Idea Exploration Shortlist

Date: exploratory research pass
Scope: global-first app ideas with existing users, public competitors, and a narrow MVP that can plausibly ship in <= 1 month.

## Criteria
- Existing user group
- Existing competitors / benchmarks
- Global relevance
- MVP fit in <= 1 month
- Narrow enough to avoid platform creep

## Shortlist (10 candidate spaces)

| # | Problem space | Market | Competition | MVP fit | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | Mobile document scanner → clean PDF/OCR | 5 | 5 | 5 | Strong demand, very crowded, commodity risk |
| 2 | Receipt / invoice capture → expense export | 5 | 5 | 4 | Clear willingness to pay, strong B2B/B2pro signals |
| 3 | Photo-to-report generator for field work | 4 | 4 | 4 | Good wedge if narrowed to one vertical |
| 4 | Frontline safety / compliance inspections | 5 | 5 | 4 | Massive real market, crowded, best in a narrow niche |
| 5 | Shift handover / daily operations logbook | 4 | 3 | 5 | Best MVP wedge so far; narrow and operationally useful |
| 6 | Fleet / equipment inspection and maintenance notes | 5 | 4 | 4 | Strong business need, slightly more domain complexity |
| 7 | Weekly review assistant for knowledge workers | 4 | 4 | 3 | Habit-forming, but can be approximated by notes/templates |
| 8 | Habit reporting with accountability | 5 | 5 | 4 | Proven market, but heavily crowded |
| 9 | Personal time / focus logging with weekly audit | 5 | 5 | 3 | Useful, but privacy + complexity can grow quickly |
| 10 | Privacy-first file transform utility | 5 | 5 | 5 | Extremely MVP-friendly, but differentiation is hard |

## What looks most promising for deeper research

### Best balance of market + MVP scope
1. **Shift handover / daily operations logbook**
2. **Photo-to-report generator for field work**
3. **Receipt / invoice capture → expense export**
4. **Privacy-first file transform utility**

### Best if the goal is maximum speed to ship
1. **Privacy-first file transform utility**
2. **Mobile document scanner → clean PDF/OCR**
3. **Shift handover / daily operations logbook**

### Best if the goal is stronger monetization potential
1. **Receipt / invoice capture → expense export**
2. **Photo-to-report generator for field work**
3. **Frontline safety / compliance inspections**

## Working interpretation

- The market is real in all 10 spaces.
- The most crowded areas are generic utility and general habit/time tracking.
- The most plausible one-month products are narrow workflow tools, not broad platforms.
- The best candidate spaces are the ones with **repeated cost**, **clear payer**, and **simple workflow ownership**.
- If we continue exploration, the next best step is to benchmark 3–4 competitors in the top 3 candidate spaces.

## Suggested next research step

Deep-dive only into these 3 first:
- Shift handover / daily operations logbook
- Photo-to-report generator for one vertical
- Receipt / invoice capture for freelancers or micro-SMBs

That keeps the search grounded in existing users, existing competitors, and a one-month MVP boundary.
